She will not lead with a number. Do not switch her to the other page mid-sentence.

Order: growth meeting → one proof she already does → one next step → one bridge → a date.

Bridge, if she freezes: "As the role grows, I want my pay to grow with it." Then stop. Then the date.

Listen for: "I'll do anything," "I don't want to talk about money," no date, "I think maybe," "sorry to ask."

Her proof, in her words, not yours. One real task is enough.
