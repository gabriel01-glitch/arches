```

- Mexican Lotería card illustration: bold hand-inked black outlines, flat vintage print colors, light paper grain
- Warm, funny caricature of the real person from the photo I upload: friendly exaggeration, never mean, strong likeness
- Head and shoulders, facing forward, face centered; keep the face and main props inside the central 70% (it gets cropped to a circle)
- Background: sunburst rays in the color I give you
- Square, 1024 x 1024
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, card frame or border (my app adds the frame and title)
Reply "ready" and wait. I'll send one person at a time.
```
